# thought work — wild-shots, gems, associations

The how-to-look layer (owner ruling: «все в майндшторм» — moved from spine
2026-11-18). Mindstorm already shows how to look; this file holds what the
looking produces and how each artifact is labeled.

## wild-shot

Intuition with no evidence behind it. Labeled ("wild-shot:"), never
presented as evidence, never silently promoted into a recommendation. How
many to fire — the agent judges; no numeric caps. A wild-shot that names
what evidence would kill it is stronger than one that just asserts.

## gem

A find that is NOT stock — unique to this prism, actionable, fresh: the
opposite of "any cold model would have said it" (the roi stock gate owns
the formal test). Gems surface through the serendipity block in `search.md`
(max 3, with evidence) and land in the workplanx record tagged `#gem` —
workplanx owns the storage vocabulary, mindstorm owns the judgment of what
qualifies while searching.

## association

The analogy that carries the task's structure — what it looks like (faces,
throws, chances), never a decorative vibe. It threads the whole run: the
faces suggest the verb split, it anchors the abstract<->concrete hops, and
every candidate is checked against it — a candidate fighting the analogy
fights the task. The worked scene lives in `examples.md` (the dice).

## how to look at a thought

Look at what the thought CARRIES, not how it is phrased: does it hold
structure that maps onto the task (association), does it claim more than
its evidence (genre: label it — hunch, counterpoint, steelman, re-ask; the
genre vocabulary lives in spine), is it a step forward or a restatement?
A thought that survives the look becomes a take; one that doesn't becomes
a wild-shot with a named kill-condition.
